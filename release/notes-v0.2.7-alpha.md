**Early alpha test build.** No textures yet, interiors are blocked out, and stats are still being tuned. Feedback welcome.

![P220 Primrose](https://github.com/NicksSoftwareFun/Nuclear-Option-P220-Primrose-Aircraft-Mod/raw/main/docs/images/1_nose.png)

The P220 Primrose is a domestically built twin-engine airliner flown by Primeva Link (PLINK), here in its airborne early warning conversion. It carries a GlobalEye-style dorsal radar, a tail laser turret that shoots down missiles aimed at it, a rear weapons bay with two sides and a pylon under each wing.

It fits the medium hangar: it rolls out squashed to fit the door and grows to full height once it is clear.

### Install

You need BepInEx 5 and Blueprinter. Drop `P220Primrose.dll` into `Nuclear Option\BepInEx\plugins` and start the game. That's the whole mod, one file.

The full list is in [CHANGELOG.txt](https://github.com/NicksSoftwareFun/Nuclear-Option-P220-Primrose-Aircraft-Mod/blob/main/CHANGELOG.txt).
