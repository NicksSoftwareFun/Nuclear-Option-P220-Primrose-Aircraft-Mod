# Credits

## Game

Nuclear Option and its assets are the property of Shockfront Studios. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Shockfront Studios.

The P220 Primrose is built on the stock Medusa setup. Its exterior model is original. At load time Blueprinter points the bundle at the game's own shaders, effects, sounds, weapons and pylons.

The bundle also carries copies of a few stock parts: the Medusa flight deck interior, crew and canopy glass, and the Spearhead's laser turret. They remain the property of Shockfront Studios. This repository does not contain them.
