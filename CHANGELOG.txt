# Changelog

## 1.0.2
- AI landings: the AI no longer stalls short of the runway or bounces and goes around after touchdown. It flies the approach faster (the reversers stop it on the runway), stays down once the wheels touch, and holds the nose down on the landing roll.
- Thrust reversers only open with weight on the main wheels, so the AI no longer opens them on final approach.
- AI takeoffs: the AI keeps the nose down until about 250 km/h, which stops tail strikes.
- Centre of gravity moved forward about 0.8 m, so the aircraft no longer tips back on full thrust from a standstill.
- A P220 that finishes its landing run takes itself off the runway's landing list, so aircraft behind it on final no longer see traffic ahead and go around.

## 1.0.1
- New bonus livery: Primevan Royal Family. Gloss black with a red swoosh edged in gold, gold engine rings, gold window borders, PRIMEVAN CROWN TRANSPORT above the windows and a white and gold PALA emblem on the tail and nose (PALA only).
- Wheel hubs take their colour from the livery (gold on Primevan Royal Family, grey on the others).
- The doors on the main gear legs now take the livery.
- Lower fuel burn, close to a real airliner at cruise.
- The auto hover and other helicopter-style flight assists are off, like the stock jets. Thrust reversers now stay open on the landing roll.
- PALA livery lettering in the same typeface as the other liveries.
- The taped manual page in the cockpit no longer clips into the panel.
- Livery Bundle: all four shipped liveries, and where to paint the hub colour.

## 1.0.0
- Two new liveries: Primeva Link Service (off-white and dusty pink airliner) and PALA Air Force Grey (air force grey with panel lines and roundels, PALA only).
- Workshop livery tag `P220 Primrose`.
- Liveries now reach every outer panel: bay doors, slats, flaps, spoilers, thrust reverser doors, the laser housing and the radar.
- Rounded nose radome tip.
- Tail and elevator tips line up with their surfaces.
- The laser can no longer aim at angles where it would hit its own aircraft (1 degree margin), so the HUD never shows SHOOT for a shot it cannot take.
- Rank 2, value 95.
- Split damage model with stock-style torn panels, interior damage textures and tougher joints.
- Cabin seats and consoles, crew at their stations, engine fan blur, fin fold hydraulics, gear lever that moves with the gear.

## 0.3.28-alpha
- First livery, "P-LINK Demonstrator", with colour, normal, metallic, ambient occlusion and damage maps. The skin is double-sided like the stock aircraft.
- Internal structure (frames, stringers, floor beams, spars, ribs) and engine cores that show through damage, in the stock structure and engine materials.
- New flight deck: side sticks, sliding thrust levers, rudder pedals that slide with yaw, four displays on the P220's own tactical page, warning lights and a taped maintenance page.
- THORNE Airborne Laser replaces the old turret. It sits in a scooped hood on the tail cone and covers everything below and behind the aircraft, with a rest pose, HUD hints and an aiming box. It never fires through its own aircraft.
- Capacitor Pack option for the rear bay (adds 75% to the laser's capacitor).
- Dorsal radar renamed SpectralEye AEW Radar.
- The hangar squash is replaced by a folding fin. It folds up 40 m after leaving the hangar.
- Thrust reversers on both engines, working only on the ground with the gear down.
- Forward door arm, door outlines, gear wells and gear mechanism, cargo bay with a side cargo door, over-wing exits.
- Own map and selection icon and own damage diagram.
- Wing pylons: single and double air-to-air, anti-radiation, heavy air-to-ground and anti-ship missiles, Genie, rocket pod, smoke pod and jamming pod. There are no triple racks, Scimitars or Tusko.
- Flight model retuned (lift, pitch authority, AI speeds).
- Triangle count cut to about 140k.
- Breaking: the plugin GUID is now nick.nuclearoption.p220primrose and the aircraft key is P220Primrose. Missions saved with 0.2.7-alpha no longer find the aircraft.

## 0.2.7-alpha
First public test build.
- Flyable P220 Primrose airborne early warning conversion with a GlobalEye-style dorsal radar.
- Tail defensive laser turret (120 kW, 135 degrees either side, missiles aimed at the aircraft only), powered by a 600 kJ capacitor.
- Rear weapons bay with Bay Left and Bay Right hardpoints and one pair of doors; one wing pylon per side.
- Radar jammer and flare dispenser.
- Forward doors open on the ground and are jettisoned for the crew to bail out (no ejection seats).
- Windscreen and ten rows of cabin windows into blocked-out flight deck and crew cabin; forward, rear and over-wing door outlines; belly cargo door.
- Squashes to fit the medium hangar door and grows to full height after leaving the hangar.
- Landing gear breaks on hard landings and rough ground like the stock aircraft.
- The laser is the last weapon in the cycling order.
